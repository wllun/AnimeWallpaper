# Content and Licensing Guidelines

## Important principle

Anime titles, characters, logos, and official artwork are usually protected by copyright and/or trademark. A public app should not redistribute artwork merely because it can be found online.

## Acceptable content sources

- Original artwork commissioned for the app with written distribution rights.
- Artwork submitted by creators who accept clear publishing terms.
- Properly licensed content from rights holders or authorized libraries.
- Public-domain or Creative Commons artwork whose license permits the intended use, with attribution where required.

## Avoid

- Scraping image search engines, fan sites, social media, or wallpaper sites.
- Removing signatures or watermarks.
- Using official franchise logos as the app's own branding.
- Implying endorsement by an anime publisher or rights holder.
- Publishing AI-generated work that closely copies a known artist's style or reproduces recognizable protected characters without permission.

## Metadata required for every wallpaper

- Internal wallpaper ID.
- Artwork title and category tags.
- Artist or rights-holder name.
- Original source URL or contract reference.
- License name and license URL or agreement record.
- Whether attribution is required and the exact attribution text.
- Date added and date rights were verified.
- Territory, expiration, and commercial-use restrictions where applicable.

## Category naming

Franchise names such as Pokémon, Digimon, or Bleach may be useful as search/category metadata, but their use should be reviewed before release. Avoid stylized official logos in navigation; use the app's own typography.

## Moderation checklist

- Confirm ownership or license before publication.
- Reject explicit sexual content, especially content involving young-looking characters.
- Reject hateful, abusive, or illegal material.
- Check that artwork is suitable for the selected age rating and store policy.
- Provide a rights-holder takedown contact and a documented removal process.

## Store listing and legal pages

Before launch, prepare:

- Privacy policy.
- Terms of use.
- Copyright/takedown policy.
- Content attribution page.
- Clear statement that the app is unofficial when appropriate.
- Age rating and content disclosures for each app store.

This document is product guidance, not legal advice. Obtain qualified legal review before distributing franchise-related content commercially.
